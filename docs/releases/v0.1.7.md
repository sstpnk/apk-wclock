# WClock v0.1.7

## Highlights

- Photo wall focus mode: tap a photo to pause the wall and expand that image to the largest size that fits the screen without stretching.
- Tap anywhere again to collapse the focused photo and resume the normal photo wall cycle.
- Photo wall stability: retired views are removed from the active set before fade-out, preventing repeated fade scheduling that could show missing or white cards.
- Decode safety: stale asynchronous bitmap results are discarded after the renderer is cleared or recycled.
